# SmoothCap

Play your PS5, Xbox or Switch on your PC through a USB capture card, and see 30 fps games at up to 60 fps with NVIDIA Smooth Motion (RTX 40 or 50 series).

One click: pick your capture card, press START. SmoothCap sets up NVIDIA for you. Low delay, fullscreen, game sound, screenshots, and it works with OBS.

**Free 20-minute trial, full version $4.99:** https://ahar0ns.itch.io/smoothcap

This repo only holds `latest.txt`, the newest version number. SmoothCap reads it at launch so everyone stays up to date.
